To export the database:
seyon@seyon:/usr/bin$ mongodump --host localhost:27017 --db openstack --out /home/remlab/Desktop/

To zip the directory:
remlab@vc-ps-chewy-10:~/Desktop$ tar -czvf /home/remlab/Desktop/openstack.tgz /home/remlab/Desktop/openstack/


To restore the database:
mongorestore --host localhost:27017 --db yourNewDatabaseName /path/to/yourBackupDir/yourDatabaseName
C:\Program Files\MongoDB\Tools\100\bin>mongorestore --host localhost:27017 --db openstack C:\Users\ozeabala\Downloads\openstack

To login to database:
seyon@seyon:/usr/bin$ mongosh --host localhost:27017
